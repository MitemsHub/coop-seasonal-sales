Before (today)

  • Saves silently — members don't know it persisted
  • No way to clear; remove line by line
  • Text-only line items, hard to scan
  • No order instructions for the branch
  • On phones the totals sit below the items — scroll past them to submit
  • One tap submits with no final look

After (this sample)

  • "Saved just now" pulse builds trust in persistence
  • Clear cart with one tap
  • Thumbnails + demand whispers per line
  • Delivery note with quick-fill chips
  • Pinned total + Review on mobile — submit is always one thumb away
  • Review sheet before committing, then a done toast + print slip

Try it

  • Adjust the steppers — totals, interest, and remaining-limit react
  • Switch Cash ↔ Loan — the interest tile and limits swap
  • Pump quantities past ₦250k — the over-limit box shakes in and disables submit
  • Tap a note chip — it fills the note; it rides into the review
  • Hit "Review & Submit" — review sheet → confirm → toast → print
  • Toggle 🖥 Desktop — summary sticks beside the items
Every control in the sample is live · toggle theme and device above · no app code touched
