Why it winsCheckout context never leaves the screen. The cart rail stays pinned beside the grid, so members see running totals while they shop — the pattern supermarkets use at the till.

Why it winsMaximum products per screen. Four columns on desktop, two tight ones on mobile; price chips overlay the image so nothing needs its own row. Built for repeat, fast shoppers.
Key changeTap-to-add instead of steppers. One tap adds, the button flips to a confirmation, and a slim cart bar slides up at the bottom — minimum taps between wanting and ordering.
Trade-offMember details are reduced to a status bar. Savings/loan breakdowns move to a detail screen. Great for a power-user cohort; needs a trust fallback for new members.
