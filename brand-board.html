Why Harvest wins

Your product is food — groceries and livestock. Green carries the cooperative movement's own identity, signals fresh produce and agriculture natively, and stays calm at depth. The one objection (green vs. the success-green status) is solved systemically, not cosmetically: approve actions use the brand primary, and green is reserved for status badges only — which is what the Mitems color chapter demands anyway. Burgundy is the strongest runner-up if you want institution-first gravitas; Sovereign is the safe floor. Say the word and I'll apply your pick to the live app.
